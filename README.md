#  Lost and Found Web-Based Management System for Strathmore University

A centralized, secure, and user-friendly web platform designed to streamline the reporting, searching, and recovery of lost and found items within the Strathmore University campus. 

This system replaces fragmented manual processes (security office logs, WhatsApp groups) with a digital solution featuring a **4-stage claim verification lifecycle**, **actionable real-time notifications**, and **dual claim pathways** (Direct and Match-Based).

---

##  About the Project

Losing personal belongings on a busy university campus is a common occurrence, but recovering them is often frustrating and inefficient. The current manual processes at Strathmore University rely heavily on physical inquiries and informal communication, leading to delayed recoveries and unverified claims. 

This project introduces a web-based Lost and Found Management System that allows students and staff to report items, upload images, search the database, and submit ownership claims. Administrators are equipped with a robust dashboard to verify claims, manage matches, and track recovery statistics, ensuring items are returned to their rightful owners securely.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Student & Staff Portal
- **Item Reporting:** Easily report lost or found items with detailed descriptions, categories, and image uploads.
- **Advanced Search & Filter:** Query the database using keywords, categories, and locations to find missing belongings.
- **Dual Claim Pathways:** 
  - *Direct Claims:* Claim a found item directly from the search results.
  - *Match-Based Claims:* Respond to system/admin-generated matches.
- **Proof of Ownership:** Submit secure, detailed ownership proofs during the claim process.
- **Actionable Notifications:** Receive clickable, real-time alerts for match discoveries, claim approvals, and rejections.
- **Personal Dashboard:** Track the status of all submitted reports and claims in one place.

### 🛡️ Administrator Portal
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Visualize system health with charts for claims overview, recovery rates, and item categories.
- **Claim Verification:** Review ownership proofs and manage the 4-stage claim lifecycle (`Pending` ➔ `Approved` ➔ `Collected` ➔ `Returned`).
- **Match Management:** Manually link lost and found items to suggest potential recoveries.
- **Data Export:** Export item and claim records to CSV for offline reporting.
- **User Management:** Oversee registered students, staff, and system roles.
